How I work

Three habits, each learned with a real audience.

Learn their world first

Before writing a word for Penumbra's reps, I sat in on their training and talked with about 25 of them. You can't explain value to someone until you know what they care about.

Say it in their words

A family in a waiting room doesn't need "PACU." A rep doesn't need a whole trial paper. I translate without dumbing down, keeping what's accurate and dropping what isn't useful.

Show it, don't just tell it

Hands-on naloxone training, a working prototype, a one-page rep briefing. People believe what they can see and try.

From the hospital floor to go-to-market

I spent years in healthcare as a scribe, medical assistant, patient care tech, and hospital volunteer. Over and over, I was the person explaining something complex to someone who needed it but didn't speak the language: patients, families, volunteers, later sales reps.

I also watched good technology fail because nobody explained it well to the people using it. That's the work I want to do: help people see what a product can do for them, wherever they're coming from. I'm technical enough to build (Next.js, and AI/ML study at UT Austin), which helps me understand the products I explain.

I'm based in San Francisco and looking for product marketing and go-to-market roles, including field and customer marketing, in AI, data, and enterprise software. Long term, I'm building toward product management.
